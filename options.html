Sync to Google Drive

Back up your notes and keep them in sync across all your devices.

Sync notes to your Google Drive and keep them synced across all your devices. Additionally, you'll be able to access them from any device using the Pagenotes Web App.

Security Note: Your data stays only on your device and in your own Google Drive. Nobody else has access to it.

Sync Status:

Web access: pagenotes.app

Note: You're using an old client for sync. For continuous operation, it's highly recommended you switch to the new client by stopping the sync and setting it up again. More Info

Notes Not Showing On Their Pages
These notes were saved under an older address format.
Page Notes now files YouTube and Amazon notes under one tidy address per video or product. These notes were saved before that, under a longer address. They are safe and still open if you return to that exact address, but they no longer show up when you reach the same page any other way. Move a note to file it under the new address.
Notes that want the same address
Each group below would end up at one address, so moving them would mean writing one note over another. Merge combines a group into a single note and keeps all of their text. To keep just one instead, delete the others from All Notes.
